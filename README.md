Ember Duel is an original turn-based card battler for the make.fun "Make the best card game" bounty. You are the Ember Duelist, facing Ash, the Cinder Rival, across a felt table: each of you has 30 life, a hand of 5 cards drawn from a 24-card deck, and 3 energy a turn to spend on Flame attacks, Tide shields, Grove heals, and Storm tricks. The rival telegraphs its next move at the start of your turn — a heavy strike, a wall, a mend — so every hand is a decision about what to counter, and playing two Flame cards in one turn chains bonus damage onto the second. Shield absorbs damage, the discard pile reshuffles when the deck runs dry, and the duel ends the moment either life total hits zero.

## How to play

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser (or serve `game/` with any static server). No network, no build step.

- **Mouse:** click a card in your hand to play it; click **End Turn** to pass.
- **Keyboard:** `1–5` plays the card in that hand slot, arrow keys move the selection, `Space` or `Enter` ends the turn, `Enter` starts from the title screen, `R` plays again after a win or a loss.
- **Goal:** reduce the rival's 30 life to 0. Watch the red intent banner — shield up when a big strike is coming, burst damage when the rival is about to mend.
