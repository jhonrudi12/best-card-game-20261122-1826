EMBER DUEL

A turn-based card battler. Outplay Ash, the Cinder Rival — read the move it telegraphs, chain your Flames, and take its 30 life before it takes yours.

1–5 / Click — play a card from your hand

3 energy a turn · 5 cards in hand · 30 life each
Two Flame cards in one turn chain: the second deals bonus damage.
